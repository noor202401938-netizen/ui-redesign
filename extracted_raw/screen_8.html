You · For

Homework takes up time we need to rest, play and be with family. After a full day at school, our brains are tired. Many students just copy answers to get it done. Banning homework would give us more sleep and time to learn in other ways.

Homework lets you practise what you learned, and practice makes it stick. It also teaches you to plan your time and work on your own. Without it, teachers would have less time in class to cover everything. A little homework, done well, can help more than it hurts.
